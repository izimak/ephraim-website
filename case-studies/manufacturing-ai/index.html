The opportunity

One person, one week, a mountain of manual work

A precision manufacturer came to Ephraim Solutions with a simple ask: how do we make what Debbie does faster — using both traditional automation and AI? Debbie's role was essential, but it ran almost entirely by hand, and with her retirement approaching, that knowledge risked walking out the door with her.
Two processes consumed her week. Certificates of Analysis (CoAs) had to be processed one at a time. And lab results arrived by email, which Debbie tracked across a maze of spreadsheets and folders before manually re-entering and updating the data in NetSuite.
All told, it took roughly 40 hours a week — a full-time job in manual data handling, and a single point of failure the company couldn't afford to lose. (Client name withheld at their request.)
One AI companion. Two processes. A week's work in an afternoon.
AI + Automation · Built alongside NetSuite
We didn't just make the work faster. We made sure it doesn't leave when Debbie does.
— Operations Leader, Manufacturing client (name withheld)
The approach

An AI companion that works inside their NetSuite ERP

Ephraim Solutions studied the business and the role, then built a fully integrated AI system that understands their existing NetSuite ERP — an AI companion that does what Debbie used to do, working directly against the systems the company already runs on.
01
Automated CoA Processing — Certificates of Analysis are ingested and processed automatically, no longer handled one at a time.
02
Lab-Result Intake — results that used to arrive by email are captured, tracked, and structured — no more spreadsheet-and-folder maze.
03
NetSuite Sync — data flows straight into NetSuite, ending the manual re-entry and updates that ate the week.
04
Traditional + AI Automation — rule-based automation for the predictable steps, AI for the judgment ones — matched to the real work.
Rather than replace NetSuite, Ephraim built alongside it — an AI companion tuned to the company's actual processes, so the automation fits the business instead of forcing the business to change.
The impact

A 40-hour week, done in an afternoon

The work that once took Debbie about 40 hours a week now runs in roughly 2 to 3. Same output, a fraction of the time — CoAs and lab results processed and synced into NetSuite automatically.
Just as importantly, the company is ready for Debbie's retirement. The platform captures how the work is done, giving them a foundation to train her replacement while cutting the role's workload dramatically.
