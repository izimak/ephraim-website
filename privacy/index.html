Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Ephraim Solutions ("Ephraim," "we," "us") is a business name of MakAnalytics LLC, based in Denton, Texas. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, fill out one of our forms (including forms on Facebook and Instagram), or talk with us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, company name, job title, and answers you provide on our forms, such as your company's size, revenue range, or the software you use today.
  • Information from conversations: notes from calls, emails and text messages with you.
  • Website and ad information: basic technical data such as pages visited, browser type and approximate location, collected through cookies and tools like the Meta Pixel and Google Analytics. This tells us which ads and pages are working.
  • Business information from public and commercial sources: company details and business contact information from public websites, licensing records and business data providers.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request and contact you about our services by phone, email or text.
  • To prepare proposals and deliver work you've asked us for.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.
  • To meet legal obligations and keep our systems secure.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number, we may text you about your inquiry. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. We do not share or sell mobile numbers or text-messaging consent to third parties for their marketing purposes.

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as our CRM and messaging platform, email and calendar providers, and advertising platforms like Meta and Google, and only to the extent they need it to provide those services. We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we have a business relationship or a legitimate reason to, and then delete or anonymize it.

Your choices

  • You can unsubscribe from our emails using the link in any email.
  • You can stop text messages by replying STOP.
  • You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us below.
  • You can limit cookies through your browser settings, and manage ad preferences in your Facebook, Instagram and Google account settings.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
